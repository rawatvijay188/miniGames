Mini Games Arcade · Last updated: 28 June 2026

Short version: Mini Games Arcade does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal information. The app has no accounts, no ads, and no analytics. All game data stays on your device.

This Privacy Policy explains how the Mini Games Arcade mobile application (the “App”) handles information. The App is a free collection of casual and casino-style mini-games played with virtual coins only. The virtual coins have no real-world value and cannot be purchased, cashed out, or exchanged for real money. There is no gambling with real money of any kind.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personally identifiable information. The App does not ask you to create an account or sign in, and it does not request your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, or any device identifiers.

2. Information Stored On Your Device

To let you keep playing where you left off, the App stores a small amount of game data locally on your device using your device’s standard local storage. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to us or to anyone else. It includes:

  • Your virtual coin balance and daily-bonus streak.
  • Local high scores for games that track them.
  • Any nickname you choose to type into a game leaderboard (optional, stored only on your device).

You can erase all of this data at any time by clearing the App’s storage in your device settings, or by uninstalling the App.

3. Information We Do NOT Collect or Use

  • No personal or contact information.
  • No location data.
  • No advertising or advertising identifiers.
  • No third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No cookies for tracking.

4. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party advertising, analytics, or social networking services, and it does not share data with third parties. If this ever changes in a future version, this policy will be updated before that feature ships.

5. Children’s Privacy

The App contains simulated casino-style games and is intended for users aged 17 and older. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

6. Data Security

Because game data is stored only on your own device and is never sent to a server, there is no transmission of your data for us to secure. Protecting the device itself remains subject to your device’s own security settings.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
